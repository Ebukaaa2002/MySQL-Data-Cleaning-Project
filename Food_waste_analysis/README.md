# Global Food Waste Analysis (2018-2024)

## About the Project
As a Data Analyst working for an international sustainability organization, I was 
tasked with analyzing global food waste data, uncovering key trends, assessing the 
economic and environmental impact, and suggesting data-driven solutions to reduce 
food wastage.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle — Global Food Wastage Dataset by Atharva Soundankar
- **Period:** 2018 to 2024
- **Size:** 5,000 rows, 8 columns
- **Countries:** 20 countries
- **Food Categories:** Prepared Food, Beverages, Bakery Items, Fruits & Vegetables, 
  Meat & Seafood, Dairy Products, Frozen Food, Grains & Cereals

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Plotly, Scikit-learn)
- Jupyter Notebook

## What I Did

### Data Cleaning
The dataset was already clean — no missing values, no duplicates. 
All data types were correct and ready for analysis.

### Exploratory Data Analysis
- Analyzed food waste trends from 2018 to 2024
- Identified top countries contributing to food waste
- Examined waste by food category
- Calculated total economic loss per country
- Visualized household vs industrial waste distribution

### Visualizations
- Interactive line chart showing food waste trend over time
- Bar chart comparing food waste by country
- Choropleth map showing economic loss by country
- Pie chart showing household vs industrial waste split
- Bar chart comparing waste by food category

### Predictive Analysis
Used Linear Regression to forecast food waste and economic loss for 2025 and 2026.

## Key Findings
- Total food waste (2018-2024): 125,308,900.36 Tons across 20 countries
- Total economic loss: $125.20 Trillion globally
- Average waste per capita: 109.46 Kg per person
- Highest wasting country: Turkey
- Most wasted food category: Prepared Food
- Household vs Industrial split: Almost exactly 50/50
- Forecast: Food waste predicted to rise to ~18.3M Tons by 2026
- Predicted economic loss by 2026: ~$18.3 Trillion annually

## Recommendations
1. Target Prepared Food reduction through better portion control and packaging
2. Focus on Turkey and Canada for immediate policy intervention
3. Equal household and industrial awareness campaigns needed
4. Investigate the 2022 drop in waste — replicate those conditions
5. Implement food redistribution programs before the 2026 spike
